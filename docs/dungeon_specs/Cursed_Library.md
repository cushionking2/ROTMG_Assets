# Cursed Library

Source files: Cursed Library - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Cursed Library Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: A portal can drop from Revenant, Creepy Beholder, Harrowed Fiend and Beholder. The guide also names Snow Troll. It always drops from Shady Sect Leader and Legion Excavator. The Cursed Library Key costs 100 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: A ruined library of scholars. Oryx and Lord Ruthven cursed it. Enemies are mutated body parts and soulless scholars.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium-high level dungeon. The guide recommends level 20 with maxed Speed and Dexterity. It calls the dungeon difficult for mid-game.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): A complex map with many branching hallways. Many hallways end in dead-end switch rooms. A guaranteed room of bookshelf walls forms a twisting maze on the path to the first boss, if it spawns.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Avalon's arena is a middle area with nine smaller square rooms linked by hallways. No sizes in tiles.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Corrupt pressure plates (switches) sit in the dead-end rooms. Activating all of them, and killing Avalon, opens a secret passage behind the spawn room. It leads to the Corruption Phantom room. The second boss stays locked even if all plates were pressed before Avalon died. Some enemies inflict Quiet, Slowed or Sick. A destructible wall hides an image of a Honey Scepter in one room type with a corrupt pressure plate. Red cracks on the floor and a Curse AoE fill the Phantom room until it dies. Players are Cursed in that room.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Reach Avalon the Archivist through the bookshelf maze. Kill him. Activate all pressure plates. Open the secret room. Kill the Corruption Phantom. Free the Realm Eye.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Not described in the pages. Phantom room has red cracks on the floor.
- Wall tiles: Bookshelves that act as walls. Tables. Destructible wall with Honey Scepter image.
- Hazards and props: Corrupt pressure plate. Book Bomb. Red cracks and Curse AoE.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Secret passage behind the spawn room. Imprisoned Realm Eye NPC.
