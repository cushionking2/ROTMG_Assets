# Spider Den

Source files: "Spider Den - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Spider Den Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal may drop from Orc King, Big Ladybug, Shambling Sludge, Werelion, Desert Werewolf and Swarm. A key costs 50 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Dirt tunnels that Arachna the Spider Queen dug for her young. Music: Crits.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Low level. It is for level 5+ characters. Many weak enemies. No party size is given.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Modest cavern of circular rooms joined by short hallways. It is linear. No branching paths.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Not given.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Spider Egg Sacs hatch when destroyed or when a player gets close. Green sacs release groups of Green Den Spider Hatchlings. Cobwebs on the floor slow movement. The boss room has no cobwebs. Many hatchlings can cause lag. A Spider Webbed Chest may open at 50% HP and self-destruct. It then spawns spiders (not Green hatchlings).
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Pass through the rooms in one line. Arachna waits in a large circular chamber at the end.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Dark brown floor. Cobwebs on the floor. Scissors may appear on the floor (rare).
- Wall tiles: Dark brown walls with small cobwebs.
- Hazards and props: Spider Egg Sac (several colors). Cobweb. Spider Webbed Chest.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Spider Den portal. Spider Den Key.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Healing Ichors from Arachna, the spiders (not hatchlings) and Spider Webbed Chests. Poison Fang Dagger. Spider Shuriken. Spider's Eye Ring. Eggshell Spider Pet Skin (rare, from the boss). Quests (Beginner pool): The Spider Queen, Run the Table.
