# Lost Halls

Source files: "Lost Halls - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Lost Halls Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal always drops from Bloodroot Heart, Ravenous Rot, and Lost Sentry. They spawn in the Sanguine Forest. The Lost Sentry can also spawn in the Runic Tundra. The Lost Halls Key costs 200 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Huge underground research network built to reclaim the realm from Oryx. Abandoned. Unethical tests took place there. Teleportation is disabled. Music: Lost Halls.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Endgame. One of the Exaltation dungeons (boosts Defense). Guide recommends maxed ATT, DEF, SPD, DEX, optionally Life and Vitality. Recommended group is about 20-30 max. Too many players makes HP Scaling hard. Solo is possible. Most runs are organized on Discord. All enemies have HP scaling.
- Time or wave limits: No dungeon timer. Agonized Titan activates 45 seconds after 3 flames are collected. Spectral Sentry timing is listed in Special mechanics.
- Sub-areas: Cultist Hideout (boss Malus) and The Void (boss Void Entity). They have their own pages. These pages are not in the inputs.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure: Procedurally generated grid of square rooms joined by hallways. The grid is 8x8, 8x9, 9x8, or 9x9 rooms. Each room holds one enemy group with one Group Leader. Rooms look alike but can have random gimmicks. The boss has no quest marker until found.
- Room sizes and counts: Room tile sizes are not given. Grid is 64 to 81 rooms. The Starting Room has 1 to 4 Pillars. There are 7 dead ends. Exactly 5 of the 7 are Pot Rooms. One is the Treasure Room. One is the Pre-Boss Room. The boss room and the Marble Defender room occupy a 3x3 block of rooms. There are always 10 rooms between spawn and the Marble Colossus. The Marble Defender is room 10. The boss room is never on the map edge. It is usually far from or opposite to spawn. A loop of rooms means the main boss path. Splits mean the main path. Splits into less space lead to pots. The page says no dead ends exist as real dead ends. Any path leads to the boss, the Titan, or a Pot Room.
- Special mechanics:
  - Starting Room: Stand near the Pillars to drop their invulnerability and kill them. Each opens a path in the wall. Each Pillar has 15,000 base HP, 35 DEF, and HP Scaling.
  - Pot Rooms: Many Treasure Pots drop Tinctures, Effusions, and stat potions. Some pots spawn Evil Spirits. One pot per Pot Room holds a passive pink flame. The flame teleports to the Treasure Room boss. A found flame shows on the minimap and shows the Treasure Room place.
  - Treasure Room: A normal-size room. It holds only the Agonized Titan and a locked portal to the Cultist Hideout. Entry only from below. 3 of the 5 flames wake the Titan.
  - Pre-Boss Room: Holds the Marble Defender. Once found, it becomes each player's Quest. Killing it opens the wall to the Boss Room.
  - Boss Room: Large square room, much bigger than normal rooms. One entrance, blocked by the Marble Defender. The entrance seals when the boss activates.
  - Key and vault logic: No key door or vault chest is named. The Vial of Pure Darkness is the key item. It drops from Malus in the Cultist Hideout to one random player who qualified for soulbound loot. After the Colossus dies, purple streaks remain. Using the Vial there opens the entrance to The Void. Pressing the enter key on the Titan portal before the Titan dies sends the player to the Nexus.
  - Room hazards: Water, Ice, or Corruption tiles. Unwalkable tiles. Destructible walls (12,000 HP each). Invincible Marble Colossus Pillars that shoot. Invincible hostile Marble Eyes with a 4.5 tile radius: Green Slow, White Weak, Orange Daze, Red Curse, Pink Bleeding. Wall arrow shooters (150 damage arrows). Floor bomb traps (150 damage AoE, two types: one aims at players, one fires at itself).
  - Room bonuses: Friendly (lighter) Marble Pillars hit all enemies within 3 tiles: Green Slow, Orange Daze, Red Curse, Pink Bleeding. Friendly Marble Eyes (4.5 tiles) help players: Green Speedy, Orange Berserk, Pink Healing, Grey Damaging.
  - Spectral Sentry: Each time a player nears a room, a small chance exists that the Sentry appears instead of an enemy group. The Haunted Halls modifier raises the chance. It is invincible. It teleports to the nearest player or decoy every 15 seconds, twice, then despawns (45 seconds total). It gives large XP to nearby players if killed.
  - Immunities: All Group Leaders are immune to Paralyze and Stasis. All Group Members are immune to Stun. Normal enemies in Cultist Hideout and The Void have no immunities.
- Order of progress: Two objectives. (1) Vial Run: collect 3 pink flames, kill the Agonized Titan, enter the Cultist Hideout, kill Malus, get the Vial. (2) Void Run: find and kill the Marble Defender, then the Marble Colossus, then use the Vial to enter The Void and kill the Void Entity. Some groups skip all normal enemies to reach the Marble Defender ("full skip Voids").

Marble Colossus phase order (from guide):
1. Stunning boomerang rings. Wakes 4 corner Pillars.
2. Chases. Slowing boomerang rings and 4-way armor-piercing spreads.
3. Center. Spawns trios of explosive rocks. Armor-piercing slash shotguns.
4. Spiral tentacles (near instant death). Paralyzing lightning. Spears that give Unstable.
5. Circles. Parametric Unstable stars, black orbs, red grenades.
6. Center. Black spiral and explosive rocks.
7. Marble Core phase 1 (one core). Dense armor-piercing Silencing rings.
8. Chases. Paralyzing yellow rings, black spear shotguns.
9. Chases. Wider spears and Unstable stars. Invincible Colossal Rock.
10. Marble Core phase 2 (two cores, opposite spin). 3 tentacles after the cores die.
11. Two more cores. Dashes at the nearest player, then yellow Paralyze/Unstable spinners.
12. Center. Omnidirectional small black Unstable spears and stars. Hard for melee.
13. Spears, explosive rocks, silencing rings. Three invincible Colossus Rocks.
14. Marble Core phase 3 (three cores). 6-direction tentacles that change direction.
15. After the last core: Invulnerable, regains all HP, despawns Colossus Rocks, fires spinners, rotating slashes, black wheels. Then rests a few seconds.
16. Final phase at full HP. Random pick of four attacks: oscillating Colossal Balls from center, lunge then Colossal Spinner rings, erratic movement with slow Colossal Balls, chase with 4-way Colossal Blasts. Every 1/5 HP lost turns a part indigo and swaps in a void attack (4/5 Void Spears, 3/5 Void Rings and indigo Pillars, 2/5 Void Blasts, 1/5 Void rings of large bullets).
17. Death: massive nova at the center. Wait before looting.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Lost Halls floor, Lost Halls Water, Lost Halls Ice, Corruption, unwalkable tiles.
- Wall tiles: Hall walls, destructible walls (12,000 HP), the Marble Defender back wall, locked-door wall in the Starting Room.
- Hazards and props: Arrow shooters, bomb traps, hostile Marble Eyes (green, white, orange, red, pink), friendly Marble Eyes (green, orange, pink, grey), hostile and friendly Marble Pillars (green, orange, red, pink), Marble Colossus Pillars, statues in the Defender room, purple streaks after the Colossus dies.
- Other objects: Treasure Pots, pink flame, locked portal to Cultist Hideout, trapdoor, boss room door, Vial of Pure Darkness, dungeon key, portal.

## Loot
- Stat potions, top-tier gear, many untiered items, T13 weapons and armor, Lost Golem ST set (Trickster: Carved Golem Remains, Brain of the Golem, Golem Garments, Rusty Cuffs), Spectral Rogue Set Shard, Helmet Rune.
- Marble Colossus: Marble Paladin Set, Sword of the Colossus, Marble Seal, Breastplate of New Life, Magical Lodestone, Life and Mana.
- Treasure Pots: Tinctures, Effusions, stat potions. Agonized Titan: stat potions, rare Carved Golem Remains. Marble Defender also drops loot.
- Cultist Hideout: UT Necromancer set, T6 rings, no T14 armor. Void Entity: T14 armor, T13 weapons, valuable UTs.
- Guide notes the Vial drops for one random player only.
