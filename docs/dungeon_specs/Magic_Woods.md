# Magic Woods

Source files: Magic Woods Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html (no main page saved)

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal is guaranteed from Ent Ancient. It may drop from Ent God (Abandoned City), Sunflower Sentinel (Floral Escape), Pumpkin Brute (Haunted Hallows), Evergreen Sprite (Sprite Forest), Ocean Spirit (Shipwreck Cove). The Magic Woods Key costs 50 in the Nexus. Drop frequency is similar to Toxic Sewers.
- Theme and setting: A fairy forest with fountains, grass paths, and many fairy enemies.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Easy midgame dungeon. Recommended: Level 20. The guide suggests a small group. Exact party size is not stated.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Relatively small layout with many dead ends.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Not given.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Enchanted Fountain Water heals but gives Hexed and slows movement. Grass paths are safe. The boss room may generate close to spawn.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Move through rooms to the boss room. Fountain Spirit is the boss.

## Enemies
| Name | Role (regular / miniboss / boss) | Key attacks or behavior | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lesser Fairies | regular | Each has a simple unique attack. Some shots pierce armor. | Found in groups near Standard or Greater Fairies. Kill them to damage their spawners. |
| Standard Fairies | regular | Chase players. Some try to sit on players. | Stay near Greater Fairies. |
| Greater Fairies | regular (strongest) | Spawn smaller enemies. Charge, circle, or sit on players. | The page names Rayr: it sits on players, then explodes into several armor-piercing shots. Other individual names are not listed. |
| Fountain Spirit | boss | Starts hidden in a central fountain. Throws 4 AOE grenades, alternating cardinal and intercardinal. After damage the fountain opens, the boss turns Invulnerable, grows, heals, then picks 1 of 5 attacks. It returns to the center between phases. | Four small fountains at the intercardinal directions spawn random dungeon enemies all fight long. |
| Ent Ancient | portal source | Spawns minions in a growth phase. | Outside the dungeon. |
| Ent God, Sunflower Sentinel, Pumpkin Brute, Evergreen Sprite, Ocean Spirit | portal source | Ent God: predictive shotguns. Ocean Spirit: never chases, extra spread if you get close. | Outside the dungeon. |

The five Fountain Spirit phases: (1) chase and circle with wide spike arcs, like Limon the Sprite Goddess; (2) circle with moderate-damage arc shots; (3) stationary, circle shots plus aimed Armor Broken shots of 0 damage; (4) stationary, aimed radial Unstable bursts plus zig-zag parametric shots; (5) stationary, slow high-damage parametric shots plus aimed wave-like spreads.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Grass pathways. Enchanted Fountain Water.
- Wall tiles: Not described.
- Hazards and props: Large central fountain, four small fountains, Enchanted Fountain Water. The boss fountain armor-pierces when walked through.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Magic Woods Key, portal, loot bags.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Full Spirit Mystic set: Spirit Staff, Enchantment Orb, Woodland Robe, Fairy Ring. Full Horticultural Huntress set: Bergenia Bow, Lifebringing Lotus, Hollyhock Hide, Chrysanthemum Corsage. Shield of Flowing Clarity (very rare, boss). Pet skin (rare, boss). Potions of Speed and Dexterity. Fairy Dust. Fountain Spirit and the Greater Fairies have notable drops.
