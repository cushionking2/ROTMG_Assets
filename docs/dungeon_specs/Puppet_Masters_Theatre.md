# Puppet Master's Theatre

Source files: "Puppet Master's Theatre - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Puppet Master's Theatre Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal may drop from Wraith, Corrupt Sprite, Unsung Jester, Sinister Scarecrow and Leviathan. The guide also lists Sea Hag. A Theatre Key costs 50 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Theatre built for the Puppet Master, Oryx's court jester. Normal enemies are puppets of the 18 classes. Music: Marionette.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium level. Guide recommends level 20. No party size is given.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Procedural layout. Rectangular rooms are separated by destructible brown blocks. The guide compares it to the Snake Pit. Navigation is maze-like.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Not given. The boss room has a wooden stage near the top with red curtains at the north end. The audience area has two types of red carpet.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Brown blocks break in one hit. Assassin Puppet grenades can fly over walls. A treasure room may appear. Treasure rooms hold the Oryx Puppet.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Start room has one or more breakable walls. Rooms follow in random order. The Puppet Master waits on the stage at the end. Treasure rooms are optional.

Puppet Master phases from the guide: (1) Djinn stream of armor-piercing bullets from the center. (2) Slowing shotgun. (3) Stationary. Spawns Wizard, Knight and Priest Puppets. Spiral of armor-piercing bullets. (4) Five clones. The real boss alternates Armored and Invulnerable. (5) Moves to the stage. Djinn pattern. Spawns many puppets. Explodes in Paralyze bullets on death. The tips page says phase 3 bullets deal 75 to 85 damage. The tips page says the boss spawns roughly 4 clones. The guide says 5.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Wooden floor (from Guild Hall). Dark red carpet (from Manor of the Immortals). Two red carpets in the audience area. Golden floor tiles in the start room. T-shaped dark red carpet in treasure rooms.
- Wall tiles: Simple brown walls. Destructible brown blocks. "Fireplaces". Red curtains (the only original tile).
- Hazards and props: Four golden figurines on each side of the start carpet. Large candelabra in treasure rooms. Wooden stage.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Theatre portal. Theatre Key.
