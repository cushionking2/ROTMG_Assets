# Haunted Cemetery

Source files: "Haunted Cemetery - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Haunted Cemetery Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: Headless Horsemen (Haunted Hallows) and Lantern Holders (Dead Church) always drop the portal. The Cemetery Key costs 100 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Cemetery for dead heroes. Skuld, master of the afterlife, lives there. Teleportation is disabled. Music: Cadaver.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium-level dungeon. Guide recommends level 20 and maxed Speed and Dexterity. Large groups are recommended. No party limit is given.
- Time or wave limits: Each of the first two stages has 4 waves, then a boss. A wave also advances after enough time passes. The pages give no number of seconds.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure: Static map. Three stages in three rooms: the Gates, the Graves, the Mausoleum. Each room is a square arena. Each is slightly bigger than the one before.
- Room sizes and counts: Three rooms. Tile sizes are not given. Pumpkins stand outside the cemetery. Fences surround the arenas. Four obstacles stand in the corners of the Mausoleum. Four candles stand in its corners.
- Special mechanics: In stage 1 and 2, Skuld taunts "READY". A player types "ready" in chat and the waves start. Only large enemies must die to end a wave. After the waves and boss, a green portal (teleporter on the north side) leads to the next stage. There is no way back. The last stage spawns a normal exit portal. In the Mausoleum, the boss fight starts after dialogue, with no waves.
- Order of progress: Gates (4 waves, then boss), Graves (4 waves, then boss), Mausoleum (Skuld).

### Waves
- Each of stage 1 and 2 has 4 waves. Wave 4 is the boss encounter. Enemies get harder with each wave and stage.
- Large enemies spawn in fixed places, in this order: wave 1 top, wave 2 bottom, wave 3 right, wave 4 left. The guide says this holds for both the Gates and the Graves.
- The page says 4 waves spawn and the last is a boss. The guide says 4 waves then a boss. The pages do not settle if the boss is a fifth step. The spawn-side order lists only 4 sides.
- Boss choice is random, one of two per stage. The boss side tells which one: Gates, top = Troll Matriarch, bottom = Ghost Bride. Graves, bottom = Pumpkin King, top = Grave Caretaker.
- The pages do not list which enemy appears in which wave.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Cemetery ground, purple graves land (outside), arena floors for each stage.
- Wall tiles: Fences around the arenas.
- Hazards and props: Carved pumpkins (one with Oryx helmet, one with skull), graves, four corner obstacles and four corner candles in the Mausoleum (red flame turns green), statues Fate and Glory.
- Other objects: Green portal (teleporter) to next stage, exit portal, Cemetery Key.

## Loot
- Many T5 abilities, T9-T11 weapons and armor, stat potions (not Life or Mana), up to five Potions of Speed per run.
- Untiered: Amulet of Dispersion, Plague Poison, Soul's Guidance, Resurrected Warrior's Armor. Full Skuld 2 the ReGhostening Rogue ST set. Tarot Card Pack.
- Amulet of Dispersion restores 120 HP when below 20% HP, with a 1800 second cooldown.
- Ghost of Skuld, Troll Matriarch, Ghost Bride, Pumpkin King, and Grave Caretaker all drop loot. The pages do not map each item to each boss.
