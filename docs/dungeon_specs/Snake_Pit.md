# Snake Pit

Source files: "Snake Pit - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Snake Pit Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal may drop from Lizard God, Undead Dwarf God, Ogre King, Zombie Cleric, Werewolf Berserker, Flying Seahorse, Medusa and Insurgent Rebel Commander. A key costs 50 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Old temple. Serpents live in the cracked stone walls. Music: Viperidae.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Low to medium level. Guide recommends level 20. No party size is given.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Circular rooms joined by hallways. Easily destructible brown blocks fill the hallways.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Always 5 rooms between the start room and the boss room. Zero or more treasure rooms. The most found is 5. No tile sizes are given.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Indestructible Snake Grate tiles spawn Pit Snakes and Pit Vipers without end. Enemies cannot pass a one-tile gap. Treasure rooms have a red button at the top. Stepping on it fires Bleeding arrows from the walls and releases the Snakepit Guard. Treasure rooms are entered from the side only.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Break blocks and clear 5 rooms. Optional treasure rooms. Then Stheno in a large room with four walls. Parts of the walls break.

Stheno attack order: (1) pairs of blades in four directions, one aimed blade, one aimed AoE grenade. (2) Seven sets of grenades around her. She does not shoot. (3) Six-arm spiral of purple blinding orbs and an aimed AoE blast. The spiral turns one way.

Snakepit Guard: Phase 1 has 3 spreads of Snake Spit, rings of Dazed Snake Spinners, three-way Snake Balls and an AoE grenade. Phase 2 starts after an invulnerable moment. It moves up and down the room and fires Snake Spit to the sides, Snake Balls in four directions, grenades, and four rows of Snake Spinners at each end. Snakepit Dart Throwers on the sides fire Bleeding arrows.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Simple brown floor tiles. Many are cracked.
- Wall tiles: Brown walls with red and blue drawings. Different faces on some walls. Destructible brown blocks.
- Hazards and props: Snake Grate. Red button (pressure plate). Vent in the treasure room center. Snakepit Dart Throwers.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Snake Pit portal. Snake Pit Key.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Stheno drops at least two Potions of Speed. It also drops Snake Oil, Wand of the Bulwark, Snake Charmer Pungi, Snakeskin Shield, Snakeskin Armor, Snake Eye Ring, a Stheno player skin and a pet skin. Tarot Card Pack is a source. Greater Pit Snake, Greater Pit Viper and the three pythons drop items (names lost). The Snakepit Guard drops items like Stheno. Quests (Standard pool): Scout the Pit, The Snake Queen.
