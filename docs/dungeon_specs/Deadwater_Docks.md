# Deadwater Docks

Source files: "Deadwater Docks - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Deadwater Docks Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal drops from Snow Wolf and Corsair Crab (chance). It always drops from Bilgewater's Galleon, Crab Sovereign, and Legion Watchmaster. The Deadwater Docks Key costs 100 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Pirate smuggler docks. Islands and wooden platforms sit in harmful Dead Water. It is a harder version of the Pirate Cave. Music: The Hempen Jig.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: High-level dungeon. Guide recommends maxed ATT, DEF, SPD, and DEX. The pages give no party size.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure: Open map of islands, ships, rafts, and walkways. Dead Water surrounds all of them. Few walls. Furnished beige brick buildings stand on some islands. Crates, weapon stands, lamps, and buoys are props.
- Room sizes and counts: No tile sizes given. One spawn ship on a crescent-shaped island. One treasure room (always, one per dungeon). One boss ship. One Kingdom Stowaway shipwreck at a random place.
- Special mechanics: Explosive Barrels deal 120 armor-piercing damage and clear enemy groups. Two barrels always stand on the spawn island. Deadwater Cannons fire cannonballs in the boss room (two rows of cannons). The boss ship has one entrance. Walls block teleport over it. The boss room floor breaks down each phase and floods tiles with Dead Water. The treasure chest stays invulnerable until all guard enemies die. The Calamity Crab cannot break the walls of the spawn shipwreck and boss ship. It bounces off them, goes invulnerable, and resets. The dead Calamity Crab leaves sand tiles under it.
- Order of progress: Spawn ship, then islands with enemies, optional treasure room, then boss ship (hallway with two ornate side rooms, then a large square room with the boss).

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Wooden planks and walkways, beige brick floor, red carpet (boss ship), sand (under dead Calamity Crab), Dead Water, plain island ground.
- Wall tiles: Beige brick walls, ship walls and hull.
- Hazards and props: Dead Water, Deadwater Cannon, Explosive Barrels, crates, weapon stands, lamps, buoys, parrot cage, skull-shaped island.
- Other objects: Bilgewater's Booty chests (gold, silver, bronze), portal, Deadwater Docks Key.

## Loot
- The dungeon is the source of Greater Potions of Speed and Dexterity, Potions of Life, and Potions of Mana. It drops the Pirate King Warrior ST set, Pirate King's Cutlass, Wavecrest Concertina, and Big Berdtha.
- The guide names Swashbuckler's Sickle, Tricorne, Naval Uniform, and First Mate's Hook as set parts.
- The boss, Calamity Crab, Bilgewater's Booty, and Admirals, Captains, and Lieutenants appear in the drop table. The saved pages do not map each item to each source.
- The treasure chest quality depends on its metal color.
