# Forest Maze

Source files: "Forest Maze - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Forest Maze Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal drops from Forest Nymph, Goblin Mage, Skeleton, Elf Wizard, Hobbit Mage, Undead Hobbit Mage, Sandsman King, Dwarf King, Drake Baby, and Enraged Bunny. The guide places them in Low Forest, Undead Forest, Beach, and Nature Ruins. The Forest Maze Key costs 50 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Dense forest. Monsters grew large because nobody found the place for years. Music: Nyooms.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Very low level. Made for level 3+ characters. Enemies and boss have low HP and damage. A harder version exists: Woodland Labyrinth.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure: Series of rooms in a forest. Each room is a random premade setpiece. The layout is procedurally generated.
- Room sizes and counts: Not given. The boss room is a large grassy room.
- Special mechanics: Trees act as walls. Players move between rooms on dark pathways between trees. The pathways do not show on the minimap. They show as gaps in the trees when the camera rotates. The guide says to follow the right wall if lost. Moth dust and Unstable bullets slow rushing.
- Order of progress: Rooms, then the boss room. Clearing every room is unnecessary.

Boss phases from the guide:
- Early: charge attacks, moth dust trail, Mini Megamoths spawn.
- When damaged enough: she retreats to the room center and circles. She spawns two Mini Megamoth groups, one circling her and one chasing players. She stays invulnerable until all die.
- After: red flash, unavoidable Stunning nova, then chase with 4-way shotguns of Unstable bullets. Moth dust continues.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Grass, exposed dirt, dirt at the center of the boss room.
- Wall tiles: Dense trees.
- Hazards and props: Moth dust trail (Stunning).
- Other objects: Portal, Forest Maze Key.

## Loot
- Seed Sprouts (Speed Sprouts) drop from every enemy. They give a 5 second Speedy effect.
- The boss drops Bramble Bow and Cocoon Quiver, and common eggs. Bow fires three wavy shots. Quiver can Paralyze.
- Weapons and armor up to T5 drop here.
