# Undead Lair

Source files: Undead Lair - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Undead Lair Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal has a chance to drop from Skeleton Worshipper (Dead Church), Skeleton of the Damned and Ghost God (Haunted Hallows), Undead Pirate (Shipwreck Cove), Burning Bones (Risen Hell) and Flesh Golem. The key costs 50 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: A crypt that was a chapel for a lost kingdom, then a burial chamber for slain soldiers. Septavius, a ghost, raised the skeletons as his army. Music: Mortuus. An advanced version exists: Heroic Undead Lair.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Mid-level. The guide recommends level 20. The guide calls it a relatively easy midgame source of Potions of Wisdom. Party size is not given.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None given.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Rooms join in a branching layout. Rooms are round or rectangular. Treasure rooms are star-shaped.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Always 9 rooms between the spawn room and the boss room (11 including both). Treasure rooms are not counted in the guide and no sizes are given.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls):
  - Camouflaged traps sit in the floor tiles. 1 or 2 trap tiles can be in a room, usually 1 of each type. They explode when a player is within 5 spaces. They are indestructible. They can do 80+ damage per bullet at low defense.
  - Trap types: square (arc toward the player), circle (omnidirectional nova), half square (boomerang shots), cross (ring of Silenced shots).
  - Living Skeletons hide in the ground like remains, then jump out and fire a wide spread.
  - Treasure rooms are not guaranteed but have a very high spawn chance. Several can spawn. They are off the main path, never directly on it.
  - The boss room has extra side rooms that can be cleared by hugging the wall without triggering Septavius. Minions stand around him.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Spawn, 9 rooms, boss room. Optional treasure rooms with Lair Spectral Skeleton.
  - Septavius (flashes blue when changing phase): 1) Spiral bullets, 75 armor-piercing damage per bullet. He is invulnerable until the minions die. 2) Alternates a 3-armed spiral with 3 faster arms, and a ring shotgun of 12 projectiles at 160 damage each, with black arrow streams and spreads; he follows slowly. 3) Rings of white shots while wandering erratically. 4) Alternates black arrows with curving blue blasts in a cross, and a 4-armed diverging/converging tentacle spiral. 5) He turns translucent, respawns ghost minions, and fires 4 black arrows at 90 degree intervals. 6) Vulnerable again, with the black arrow cross and white rings together.
  - The 12-shot nova ("Septavigun") deals a total of 1920 to a 0-defense character. High DEF lowers it to 288 minimum (needs at least 136 DEF).
  - Lair Spectral Skeleton: fires diamond walls of shots with gaps, throws an AOE grenade at the nearest player. At 50% HP it turns Invulnerable briefly, then attacks faster.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Stone floor tiles, trap floor tiles, tiles with faces (angry face, skull, Oryx face, Creeper face), star-shaped treasure room floor.
- Wall tiles: Crypt stone walls. Not described further.
- Hazards and props: Lair traps, skulls, Ghost of Skuld statue, arrows stuck in the floor, rooms with bows, crossbows and targets, a rare monument like the Adobe Flash logo.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Undead Lair portal and key. Boss loot bags.

## Loot
- Septavius and Lair Spectral Skeleton each drop a guaranteed Potion of Wisdom for at least two people.
- Untiered: Doom Bow, Wandering Souls Spell, Ring of Skeletal Specters, Spectral Sword, Spectral Arrowhead. Hollow King Necromancer set pieces: Edictum Praetoris, Toga Picta.
- Possessed Staff Pet Skin (boss, chance). The Spectral Skeleton drops most of the boss loot except the mark, skin and spell.
- Completion gives Tunnel Rat, Hero of the Nexus and Realm of the Mad God fame bonuses. The page shows "27-35" under Dust Drops, with labels lost.
