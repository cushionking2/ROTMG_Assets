# Davy Jones Locker

Source files: Davy Jones' Locker - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Davy Jones' Locker Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: A portal can drop from Abyssal Hydra and Sunken Treasure. It always drops from Ghost Ship and Well of Souls. Davy's Key costs 100 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: The sunken wreck of Davy Jones' flagship. Ghost and zombie pirates guard it. Players do not suffocate here.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium-high difficulty. Suggested level 20 with Defense maxed. The guide recommends maxed DEF, SPD and DEX. A group is easier than a solo run.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated. After the boss dies, minions keep spawning.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): A very large layout. Many interconnected hallways with rooms branching off. The boss room is a larger, rounder room. It is always entered from the bottom.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: No sizes. Example only: with 26 rooms there would be 12 blue, 8 green, 4 red and 2 yellow door rooms (ratio 12B/8G/4R/2Y). The guide says this is an example.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): The player needs 4 keys in order: blue, green, red, yellow. The blue key is in the first room. The first room locks players in. Barrels there can be shot to move down. The blue key stands in the middle of the bottom wall. The green key is behind a blue door. The red key is behind a green door. The yellow key is behind a red door. The boss is behind a yellow door. Touching a door of an unlocked color opens it. Keys show in a custom UI at the top left. Keys stay on the UI after the dungeon. Locked doors appear as markers on the minimap. The 4 keys have unique shapes since Exalt 5.4.0.0. Extra yellow rooms give a chance of a purple bag with Ghost Rum. Any normal room without a key may hold Sunken Treasure (up to 5 treasure rooms). The boss room is octagonal with a smaller octagon of lanterns in the middle. The guide mentions back lanterns and 4 front lanterns, so 8 or more lanterns are possible. The page does not give an exact count.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Blue key, green key, red key, yellow key, then the boss room. Light all lanterns to make Davy Jones vulnerable.
