# Abyss of Demons

Source files: Abyss of Demons - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Abyss of Demons Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: A portal can drop from Blazing Sprite, Risen Hellhound, White Demon, Red Demon and Vagabond Satyr. It always drops from Legion Guard Captain. The Abyss of Demons Key costs 50 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Demon lair made by Archdemon Malphas. Red rooms, bones, lava rivers and statues.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium-level dungeon. The guide recommends level 20 with maxed Defense and Speed. An unmaxed class can finish in about 10 minutes. A solo run is possible.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): A large network of small square rooms. Very short hallways link the rooms. Dead ends exist.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: No room sizes given. The boss room is always 10 rooms from spawn.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Lava rivers cross the dungeon. Lava slows players and deals damage. Broken tiles on lava pits in hallways let players cross safely, but they slow the crossing. Bridges can hide behind tall walls. A treasure room may appear. A long red brick hallway with 4 red pillars leads north to it. Players enter it only from the south. They cannot leave until the Abyss Idol is dead, because lava blocks the exit. In the boss room, the lava seeps away after Malphas dies. Every room holds monsters.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Clear rooms toward the boss room. Kill the White Demons around the boss room. Then fight Archdemon Malphas. The Abyss Idol room is optional.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Simple red ground tiles. Broken (safe) tiles on lava.
- Wall tiles: Red wall tiles. Red brick hallway tiles for the treasure room entrance.
- Hazards and props: Abyss Lava. Piles of bones. Red pillars (4 in the treasure hallway). Statues: generic demon shape, Abyss Idol dormant, golden Night Prince Paladin-like statue, Molek, three forms of Balaam, Medusa or Stheno, Oryx helmet, Silex, turtle. Yellow bombs mark lava flood spots in the Idol fight.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): No doors or keys. Loot bags after the bosses.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Potion of Vitality. Untiered: Demon Blade, Volcanic Sheath, Berserker's Breastplate, Adamantine Ingot. Magmatic Mystic ST set: Staff of Eruption, Scorchium Stone, Molten Mantle, Ring of the Inferno. Rare pet skins from the boss. The treasure room guarantees Defense and Vitality. The boss may drop Potion of Defense. Dust drops: 33-39.
