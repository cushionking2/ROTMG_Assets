# Parasite Chambers

Source files: "Parasite Chambers - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Parasite Chambers Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal drops from Blood Ooze and Abyssal Kraken (chance). It always drops from Scout Colony (and Cold Soul, per the main page). A key costs 200 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Infested catacombs. The pages say the rooms may be an old guild hall. Music: Mist'enuse.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Very high difficulty. Maxed DEF is advised for small groups. The guide advises a group of 20 players at most. HP of large enemies scales with player count. The pages give no level range.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Rooms of varying shape joined by hallways. Destructible barriers block the hallways. The boss room is larger. Its entrance is from the sides.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Exactly 3 dead ends per dungeon (not counting spawn and boss rooms). Each dead end may be a Treasure Room. A Treasure Room is a small round room. No tile sizes are given.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Explosive barrels are in every room except the spawn and Treasure Rooms. The minimap shows them as orange dots. A player bump rolls a barrel. It explodes on a wall, an enemy, or at max range. It deals 900-1100 armor-piercing damage to enemies. It applies Armor Broken for 3 seconds and removes invulnerability. A player hit by it takes no damage but is Armor Broken for 3 seconds. The barrel respawns in the same place and flashes green when ready. Enemies see and shoot through walls.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Clear rooms and colonies. Each dead end may hold a Treasure Room. Then fight the Nightmare Colony. Killing Blob, Pterasite and Swarm Colonies first slows the boss maw.

Boss room maw: the guide names an invincible maw in the room center. It fires radial Pet Stasis bullets (10 damage each) and aimed grey bombs (Quiet). It fires slower for each small colony killed. The guide gives no object name.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Not described. The Treasure Room has candelabras.

- Hazards and props: Explosive barrel. Destructible barrier. Candelabra. Infested Chest.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Parasite Chambers portal. Parasite Chambers Key. Infested Chest. Uninfected Chest.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: The dungeon gives Potions of Mana and possibly Potions of Attack. It drops four untiered robe-class abilities. These include Recurring Terror Spell, Scepter of Devastation and Tome of Pain. It drops the full Flesh Collector set for the Assassin: Symbiotic Ripper, Parasitic Concoction, Rags of the Host, Hivemind Circlet. Nightmare Colony and Infested Chest drop the main loot. Blob, Pterasite and Swarm Colonies drop some items. The table lost the item names. The Mighty Quest pool has 3 quests: The Nightmare, Unsettling Foes, Lost and Found.
