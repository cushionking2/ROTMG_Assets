# The Crawling Depths

Source files: The Crawling Depths - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; The Crawling Depths Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal has a chance to drop from Sting Spider (Carboniferous), Blood Hound (Sanguine Forest), Spore Spreader (Floral Escape) and Critter Brood. It always drops from Legion Lead Strategist in the Realm. The key costs 100 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: An underground spider cavern. The exiled son of Arachna stole her eggs and raised an army of spider warriors in a mountain burrow. Music: Chelicerae.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: High-level dungeon. It is a much harder Spider Den. The guide recommends maxed SPD, DEX and VIT. The wiki calls it harder than Woodland Labyrinth and easier than Mountain Temple. Party size is not given. A decent group with one rusher works well.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None given.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): A cavern of roughly circular rooms joined by short hallways. It is a single chain. No branches, no wrong turns, no dead ends. Layout matches the Spider Den.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: No numbers are given. The boss room is a large circular room. The treasure room is a large circular room.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls):
  - The ground has continuous channels of web that slow movement, like water.
  - A destructible web block wall (4 webbed walls at the bottom of the boss room) blocks the boss entrance. It does not regenerate HP. The boss entrance faces south.
  - Egg Sacs spawn spiders when killed or when a player comes within about 2 tiles. Colors: Blue (Spider Hatchling), Green (Green Spider), Red (Red Spotted Spider), Grey (Grey Spider), Grey with pink spots (Grey Spotted Spider).
  - An optional treasure room may replace one room on the path. It is full of Glowing Eggs, which have small loot drop chances. A run may hold several. They are not guaranteed. A treasure room has orange stalagmites. Glowing Eggs can also spawn in normal rooms at a low rate (yellow dot on the map).
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Rooms in a chain, optional treasure room, web wall, Son of Arachna's lair.
  - Boss fight: Son of Arachna is in the center. Four colored Giant Egg Sacs sit in the corners. Many Egg Sacs line the edges. The fight starts when a player nears the center. He alternates vulnerable and invulnerable. Each egg destroyed makes him vulnerable for about 1/4 of his health. One random egg is "webbed" and can die only after the others.
  - Blue egg: removes Energized (no greater cave spiders) and the blue quieting shot.
  - Green egg: removes Speedy and the green slowing shot.
  - Red egg: removes Damaging (Armor Piercing) and the red Bleeding shot.
  - Silver egg: removes Armored (defense down by 1/3) and the purple exposing shot.
  - Tip: kill the blue or green egg first, then red or silver.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Cave dirt, web channels, spider dirt web.
- Wall tiles: Cave walls, destructible web block wall.
- Hazards and props: Egg Sacs in 5 colors, orange stalagmites, webs, Glowing Eggs, 4 Giant Egg Sacs.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Crawling Depths portal and key. Loot bags from the boss.

## Loot
- Guaranteed for at least two players: Greater Potion of Defense, Greater Potion of Wisdom, Potion of Mana. Potions of Life also drop.
- Untiered: Doku no Ken, Spider Silk Bow, Mace of the Depths, Silken Sigil.
- Treasure room Glowing Eggs can drop what the boss drops. The giant egg sacs also drop loot.
- Completion gives Explosive Journey, Epic Battles, Hero of the Nexus and Realm of the Mad God fame bonuses. The page shows "37-43" and "41-47" under Dust Drops, with labels lost.
