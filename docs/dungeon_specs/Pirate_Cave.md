# Pirate Cave

Source files: "Pirate Cave - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html", "Pirate Cave Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html"

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The portal often drops from Pirate, Piratess, Little Green Jelly, Little Blue Jelly, Little Pink Jelly, Giant Crab, Sand Devil and Goblin Warriors. It always drops from X Treasure Chest and Beached Buccaneer. A key costs 50 in the Nexus. Enemies on the Beach and part of the Undead Forest drop it.
- Theme and setting: Shipwrecked pirate hideout. Ships and islands in water, joined by wooden planks. Music: Scallywag.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Very low level. It is for level 1+ characters. It is the easiest dungeon. The pages give no party size.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Shipwrecked ships and islands with platforms. There are no walls. Players can wade through water to the boss ship.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Not given. Some islands may hold many "dancing" crabs (rare).
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Water slows the player. The boss ship has on-board cannons. Pirate paintings hang inside ships.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Clear platforms and islands for XP and gear. Then reach the large boss ship. It has red carpets and sails. Dreadstump stands at the back of the ship with stronger minions.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Wooden planks and decks. Red carpets on the boss ship. The pages do not name the island ground.
- Wall tiles: None. The dungeon has no walls.
- Hazards and props: Water. Cannons. Sails. Paintings. Crabs.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Pirate Cave portal. Pirate Cave Key. Reward drops from Dreadstump.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Pirate Rum drops often from Dreadstump and less often from the Admiral, Captain, Commander and Lieutenant. It inflicts Drunk. Corsair Ring gives +20 HP and +2 DEF. It drops in a cyan bag from Dreadstump. First Mate's Hook is a low-chance drop. It is part of the Pirate King Warrior Set. Scallywag Slurp Pet Skin is a very rare drop from Dreadstump. Quests (Beginner pool): The Pirate King, Run the Table.
