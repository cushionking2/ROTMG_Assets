# Mad Lab

Source files: Mad Lab - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Mad Lab Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: Portal drops from Banshee, Hazardous Sprite, Flying Brain, Artificial Sprite God. The Lab Key costs 100 in the Nexus. The guide says Flying Brain (Abandoned City), Banshee (Haunted Hallows), Hazardous Sprite (Sprite Forest).
- Theme and setting: A lab from a far dimension. Alchemy and technology. Dr. Terrible built the tools that make pets possible. Music: Wrongworks.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium-level dungeon. The guide calls it relatively easy midgame. Recommended: Level 20. The main page lists "31-39" next to "Dust Drops". Party size is not stated.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Rooms joined by paths. The layout varies ("Example Layout"). The wiki map is outdated as of Exalt 4.2.4.0.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Not given.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Walls block some paths and players must shoot them down. When two paths exist, the path blocked by a wall is always the right path to the boss room. One room type has several blocked paths. The wrong ones dead-end quickly. Tesla Coils (white boxes) sit in rooms. Destroying all Tesla Coils opens the barrier to the second boss. Green Lab Vats turn the player into an animal (Stunned, but Speedy). Blue Lab Vats turn the player back to human. Some rooms need the player to cross a green vat and use the blue vat on the far side. Cross-shaped rooms lined with Green Lab Vat exist. A removed room type had a grate maze over Liquid Evil with Automated Defense Turrets that fired Confused shots.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Clear rooms. Destroy every Tesla Coil. Fight Dr. Terrible. Then fight Horrific Creation in the secret room.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Grates above Liquid Evil (removed room type). Lab floor. Colors are not given.
- Wall tiles: Blocking walls that players destroy. Cage walls in the boss room.
- Hazards and props: Green Lab Vat, Blue Lab Vat, Liquid Evil, Tesla Coil, Automated Defense Turret, boss-room safety bubble, four cages.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Lab Key, portal to the Mad Lab, loot bags.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Potions of Wisdom, Attack, Defense. The full Mad Scientist Sorcerer UT set: Conducting Wand, Scepter of Fulmination, Robe of the Mad Scientist, Experimental Ring. The full Horrific Sorcerer ST set: Abomination's Wrath, Grotesque Scepter, Garment of the Beast, Horrific Claws. Tarot Card Pack. Robobuddy Pet Skin (rare, boss). The secret boss drops a guaranteed Potion of Defense. Crusher Abomination and Escaped Experiment have notable drops. The wiki tables show item icons only, so some item names are missing.
