# Cave of a Thousand Treasures

Source files: Cave of a Thousand Treasures - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Cave of a Thousand Treasures Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: A portal can drop from Sunken Soul, Silvery Narwhal, Washed-Up Captain and Djinn. The key is the Treasure Map. It comes from certain Daily Quests. The Nexus does not sell it. The Ninja level 20 gift on a new Kongregate account also gave it.
- Theme and setting: A thieves' cave full of treasure, booby traps and brown rock. Floor and wall tiles look like the Spider Den.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium-level dungeon. The wiki says level 20 unmaxed. The guide recommends level 16. It is easy.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): A fixed linear layout of 8 rooms. Enemy placement varies a little. The layout never changes otherwise.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: 8 rooms. No sizes in tiles. Room list:
  - Room 1 (start): octagonal. Three concentric square Log Trap tracks. Treasure obstacles, Treasure Pots and rocks at the edges.
  - Room 2: small. A few enemies, Treasure Pots, obstacles.
  - Room 3: hexagonal. Two parallel Log Traps, a few enemies, Treasure Pots, obstacles.
  - Room 4: long hallway with a Boulder Trap. Four small cavities in the walls give shelter.
  - Room 5: hexagonal. Enemies, Treasure Pots, small maze of treasure and rock obstacles. Treasure Mimics sit at dead ends.
  - Room 6: twisting room. Two parallel Log Traps, enemies, Treasure Pots, obstacles near the walls.
  - Room 7: twisted hexagon. Mostly Flame Traps and rocks. A few safe areas with Treasure Pots and treasure obstacles. The guide says there are 5 safe places, blocked by breakable pottery.
  - Room 8 (boss): large circular arena. The Golden Oryx Effigy stands in the center. A few obstacles and Treasure Pots. No traps.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): All traps are invulnerable. Boulder: 60 damage, endless supply from the top of the corridor, rolls down fast. Log Trap: 50 damage on contact, moves along a rail and rebounds at the end. Flame Trap: serves as floor, jets flames for a few seconds, 50 damage while flames are active, some fire faster. Flame Traps fire in the order they render in view, so slow players unsync them. Treasure Pots are breakable.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Rooms 1 to 7 in order, then the boss room. There are no minibosses.
- Note: The guide says the Boulder trap is in the 3rd room. The wiki says room 4. The two pages differ.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Brown floor tiles. Similar to the Spider Den.
- Wall tiles: Brown wall tiles.
- Hazards and props: Boulder, Log Trap, Flame Trap (traps). Log Trap rails. Piles of gold coins, jewels, chests, other treasure obstacles. Rocks. Breakable pottery. Treasure Pots.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Treasure Map (key). Chest obstacles are decoration only.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Stat potions except Life and Mana (minions drop them often, the boss uncommonly). Medium-tier gear and rings. Tarot Card Pack. Jewel Eye Katana (Shiny), the only untiered item. Mark of the Effigy. The daily quest "The Effigy" gives a Mighty Quest Chest, Loot Drop and Loot Tier Potion. Dust drops: 43-50.
