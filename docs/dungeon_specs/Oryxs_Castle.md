# Oryx's Castle

Source files: Oryx's Castle - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Oryx's Castle Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: No portal. Reach 100% Realm Score and defeat specific Quest Monsters. Oryx closes the realm. After 2 minutes, an earthquake teleports players in the realm into the castle. Players inside a dungeon are not teleported. Teleportation is disabled inside.
- Theme and setting: Oryx's island fortress. Four ports for ships. Music: Passage. In winter the Stone Guardian room floor is icy and slippery. At Halloween it becomes the Parasite Castle.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium to high-level dungeon. The guide calls it a difficult midgame dungeon. Recommended: maxed ATT, DEF, SPD, DEX. Players split into 4 teams. The main page lists "31-39" next to "Dust Drops".
- Time or wave limits, if any: The 2-minute wait before the teleport. No timer inside.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Static map that never changes. Four near-identical wings. Each player spawns on a small island port. Path: long bridge, entryway, three rectangular rooms (hallways), central courtyard, short hallway, Guardian Room. Janus's room is behind the Guardian Room.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: 4 wings. 3 rectangular rooms per wing. 1 courtyard. 1 Guardian Room. 1 Janus room. No sizes given.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): High-health dark blue walls block sections. Players must destroy them. Oryx's Living Floor guards wall barriers at the port hallways, the courtyard exit to the last hallway, and the last hallway exit to the boss room. Janus's room opens only after all Suits of Armor in the castle and the Stone Guardians die. Parasite Castle has explosive barrels. A lag spike occurs just after spawn.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Port, three hallways, courtyard, Guardian Room (Stone Guardians), optional Janus room.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Castle stone floor. Icy floor in the Guardian room in winter.
- Wall tiles: High-health dark blue barrier walls. Castle walls.
- Hazards and props: Bridges, island ports, courtyard, Quiet Bombs, red floor indicators for Keys, explosive barrels (Halloween), Stone Guardian Sword.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Portal to Oryx's Chamber, Janus portal to the Court of Oryx, Key of Light, Key of Darkness, loot bags. The old portal sprite changed in Patch X.32.4.1.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Potions of Defense. Stat potions. Ancient Stone Sword. Spiteful Scutum. Mad God's Messenger Priest ST set: Theurgy Wand, Ceremonial Merlot, Anointed Robe, Ring of Pagan Favor. Wines (Fire Water and Cream Spirit give +230). Magic Potions from Warriors. Large XP. Janus and the Stone Guardians drop notable items. Brute, Commander, and Suit of Armor drop items. White bag possible.
