# Candyland Hunting Grounds

Source files: Candyland Hunting Grounds - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Candyland Hunting Grounds Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The Candy Gnome always drops the portal. It is a rare enemy in the Abandoned City. The Candy Key costs 200 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: An edible biome. Lollipop trees, dessert obstacles, candy water.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium-level dungeon. The guide recommends level 20 with maxed Defense. Soloing is possible. Players often call for help.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None. The dungeon can be farmed without end. Enemies respawn after players leave a room. Enemies despawn when no player is near the room. A lone cloaked player causes despawn and respawn.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Round rooms joined by very short hallways. The dungeon sits in a dense impassable forest of lollipop trees.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Not given. The Cupcake appears in a small yellow room placed at random. It may be the start room.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Killing a big minion (miniboss) gives a chance to spawn the matching boss in the boss room. A quest marker shows it. The Candy Gnome taunts when a boss spawns. Bosses spawn even if players stand in the boss room. They do not attack for a few seconds and are Invulnerable at first. Only one boss is present at a time. More bosses queue up (stacking). After one of each boss type dies, the Cupcake spawns. Killing the Cupcake counts as completion. It replaces the Portal of Cowardice with a Realm Portal. The Cupcake spawns once per instance. Other bosses can still spawn after it.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Kill minibosses. Kill the bosses that spawn. Kill at least one of each of the 5 boss types. Then kill the Cupcake.
