# Manor of the Immortals

Source files: Manor of the Immortals - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html; Manor of the Immortals Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: Portal is guaranteed from Kage Kami. It may drop from Flying Vampire and Cerberus. Kage Kami and Flying Vampire live in the Dead Church. Cerberus lives in the Risen Hell. The Manor Key costs 50 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Lord Ruthven's stately mansion in a dark ocean. A vampire center for dark arts. Music: Wront.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Medium to high-level dungeon for strong characters (level 20 with defense maxed). The guide calls it fairly easy. Recommended: Level 20. The main page lists "29-37" next to "Dust Drops". Party size is not stated.
- Time or wave limits, if any: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): A large mansion with rooms joined by hallways. Each room is a random premade setpiece. The layout is procedurally generated. Branch lengths were reduced in Exalt 5.2.0.0.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Always 9 rooms between the start room and the boss room (not counting either). Bookcase structures of 4x4 exist. The boss room is large and "squareish". At least one treasure room is likely. Two or more are common.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): Some bookshelves are destructible and appear lighter brown on the minimap. They hide treasure rooms. Treasure room bookshelves have no shadow with the floor. Central bookshelves in cross-shaped rooms (purple floor, red carpet) can be destroyed. They hold a Nosferatu or a Coffin. A set of destructible bookshelves in one room type hides a lone enemy. Lightning flashes in windows. Hellhounds charge players in the start room. Vampire Bats cause a debuff (the main page says Confused, the guide says Unstable).
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Start room, 9 rooms, boss room with Lord Ruthven. Treasure rooms are optional.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Dark blue, blue-gray, or purple brick. Dark red carpet.
- Wall tiles: Dark purple stone. Dark blue walls with paintings and windows. Bookshelves (normal and destructible).
- Hazards and props: Candelabra near room edges. Bones and blood pools in prison-like rooms. Cells. Coffins. Lightning in windows.
- Other objects (chests, portals, doors, keys): Manor Key, portal, loot bags, secret chamber behind bookshelves.

## Loot
- Drops, soulbound items, reward chests: Potions of Attack. Holy Water (boss and treasure room only). Bone Dagger. Kyuuketsuki Hitogoroshi. Full Purified Priest UT set: St. Abraham's Wand, Tome of Purification, Chasuble of Holy Light, Ring of Divine Faith. Vampire Rogue ST set: Soulcursed Scythe, Vampiric Cape, Hallowed Hide, Ruthven's Rosary. Bat Egg. Vampire Lord Skin (rare). Necromancer skin. Tarot Card Pack. Coffins can drop boss loot.
