# Forbidden Jungle

Source files: "Forbidden Jungle - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html" (main page) and "Forbidden Jungle Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html" (guide, last updated Exalt 2.2.3.0, Feb 2022).

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: The guide says the portal drops only from the Great Coil Snake, found in the Nature Ruins. The main page says Great Coil Snakes always drop it and Black Bats and Earth Golems may drop it. The Totem Key costs 100 in the Nexus (main page). The pages disagree. See Gaps.
- Theme and setting: Dense jungle with primitive tribes. Residents stayed after Oryx's uprising.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Low to medium level. Made for level 10+ players. The guide calls it a fairly easy early-game dungeon and a good XP farm. A harder version exists: Secluded Thicket.
- Time or wave limits: None stated.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure: The main page says a network of roughly circular rooms linked by hallways. The guide says a mostly linear path of mud floors, bushes, and trees, with a few dead ends that split off the main path. The boss is easy to find.
- Room sizes and counts: Not given. The boss room is a large circular arena.
- Walls: Impassable tropical foliage and trees. Basilisks and Basilisk Babies can travel between the "walls" of trees. Other enemies may lurk inside the walls.
- Skips: Trickster prisms and Rogues with a Cloak of the Planewalker can teleport past rooms to the boss.
- Boss room: Four Boss Totems (totem poles) stand around the boss. Gray stone bricks are at the center.
- Order of progress: Rooms and hallways along the main path, then the boss room.

## Boss: Mixcoatl the Masked God (from the guide)
1. Phase 1: On first hit, he shoots a spiral with sword range only. A player can circle him and melee.
2. Phase 2: He moves to the center. He and the four totem poles shoot 360-degree explosions. Two safe zones exist: one close for melee, one farther for ranged classes.
3. Phase 3: He chases the player. The player can lure him out of the room.
4. A phase change happens after enough damage or enough time. The guide does not give HP or exact thresholds.
5. Rare drop: Baby Basilisk pet skin.

## Tiles and objects to find as sprites
- Floor tiles: Mud (guide), dirt and grass (main page). Gray stone bricks at the boss room center.
- Wall tiles: Dense impassable tropical foliage, bushes, trees.
- Hazards and props: Small rocks, plants, skewered skulls around the boss arena.
- Other objects: Boss Totems, Jungle Fire, portal, Totem Key.

## Loot
- Earliest dungeon with a full UT set: the Crystal Necromancer Set.
  - Staff of the Crystal Serpent (damage close to T8 staffs).
  - Cracked Crystal Skull (large radius, low damage).
  - Robe of the Tlatoani (no DEF, more MP and WIS).
  - Crystal Bone Ring (no fame bonus).
- Pollen Powder: exclusive consumable. Refills 100 MP in a radius, like Snake Oil.
- Baby Basilisk pet skin: very small chance from the boss.
- The Nature Ruins chest of the Great Coil Snake holds gear and HP/MP potions.
- The Mask Warrior, Mask Hunter, and Mask Shaman also drop from the boss table (main page).
- Item-to-source drop table: icons only. Item names for the table are lost.
