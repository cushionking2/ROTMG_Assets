# Ocean Trench

Source files: Ocean Trench Guide - the RotMG Wiki _ RealmEye.com.html (no main page saved)

## Overview
- Portal / how to enter: Portal is guaranteed from the Hermit God Encounter (Coral Reefs) and the Eye of the Storm Encounter (Shipwreck Cove). It may drop from Abyssal Squid (Deep Sea Abyss) and Ice Giant (Runic Tundra). The Ocean Trench Key costs 150 in the Nexus.
- Theme and setting: Underwater dungeon with a suffocation mechanic and sea creatures.
- Party size, difficulty, level range: Fairly difficult midgame dungeon. Recommended: maxed ATT, DEF, SPD, and DEX. The guide page is marked outdated (Exalt 2.0.3.0). A group is advised. Soloing is possible.
- Time or wave limits, if any: No timer. An air meter limits time away from vents.

## Layout and map logic
- Overall structure (rooms, corridors, open cave, grid, linear): Rooms. Details not given. The spawn area is safe.
- Room sizes and counts, if the guide gives them: Not given. Usually 2 air vents per room. The boss room has only 4 air vents.
- Special mechanics (keys, doors, waves, hazards, puzzles, destructible walls): An air meter (teal-blue bar under the character). Air vents refill it. With no air, the player loses HP faster than in lava. Paralyze and Confuse make reaching vents hard. An enemy may cover a vent.
- Order of progress (rooms, minibosses, boss): Clear rooms from top to bottom. Clear linked rooms before the boss. Fight Thessal the Mermaid Goddess.

King Alexander event: at random, or with the "Alexander's Legacy I/II" modifier, Thessal slumps over the loot bag and portal. She asks "Is King Alexander alive?". The right reply is "He lives and reigns and conquers the world". She then spawns Coral Gifts. A wrong or late reply makes her shout "You speak LIES!", attack, and heal 10,000 HP. She explodes and kills players who take loot too early.
